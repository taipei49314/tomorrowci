# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in TomorrowCI a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Standards

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent; critique ideas, not people.
- No harassment, spam, or malicious security research against contributors without consent.

## Enforcement

Report issues via the contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may warn, limit, or ban contributors for violations.
